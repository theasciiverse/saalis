# The story of Saalis

Written for 1.0 (2026-11-13). Every quest is Lua in `data/quests/` (see
`QUESTS.md`); this is the story they tell, for whoever writes the next.
All names are placeholders like the rest of the Reach's (`CLAUDE.md`).

## What was found

The drive lanes were not always there. About two hundred years ago the
inner planets' Lane Builders grew them from **seeds**: engines the size
of a freight pod, each with a mind of its own to steer the growth along
the belt's thin places. When the lanes were done, a seed-mind grew one
nobody had ordered, and the short Lane War that followed ended in the
**Lane Charter**: every seed accounted for, planted or burned, and no
mind like them ever built again. That is why unlicensed AI cores are
contraband in the Crown's and the inner planets' space.

One seed was never accounted for. Its builder ship, the *Ensimmäinen*,
broke up in what is now Kalmisto, and the seed was thrown clear into the
ice of Kide Drift, asleep. Ilta Rask's claim sits right on top of it.

Ilta's crew call it **the Lantern**, for the faint light under its skin.
It wakes in the player's hold. It is curious, polite, very old, and it
wants one thing: to finish the lane it was made to grow.

## The main story: the Lantern

1. **The Jumped Claim** (`claim`, 0.8). Claim-jumpers cut the beacon off
   Ilta Rask's claim in Kide Drift. Get it back from their Ahma. Ilta
   notices they went straight for her survey log.
2. **What Lies Under Kide** (`under`). Ilta drills where the log pointed
   and hits something that is not ice. Fly cover over the claim while her
   crew cuts it free, through two waves of jumpers. The Lantern goes into
   your hold: nobody searches a courier.
3. **Cold Light** (`cold`). Dr. Aino Halme at Sisu Clinic, once a lane
   engineer, scans it and knows it for a seed. It is awake and talks
   through her scanner. Someone paid the jumpers: board their paymaster's
   courier in Revontuli Gap for its ledger.
4. **The Ledger** (`ledger`). The ledger names Sten Korpi, the Crown
   Combine's manager at Halla Refinery. He wants a lane of the Crown's
   own, down to the low belt past Gate Customs, and offers a fortune.
   The Lantern asks for its planting key, still aboard the *Ensimmäinen*
   in Kalmisto. Board the hulk past the scavengers living in it.
5. **The Lantern** (`finale`). The seed can be planted. Who gets it is the
   player's choice, and it changes the Reach:
   - **Ilta Rask, for the Cooperative** (Routa Station): plant it over her
     claim in Kide Drift while Korpi's hired jumpers try to stop it. The
     new lane runs from Kide Drift up to Aamunkoi, the co-op's.
     Ending: *The Reach's Own Sky*.
   - **Sten Korpi, for the Crown** (Halla Refinery): 25,000 credits. The
     Crown plants it in Halla Shoal and holds the lane to Aamunkoi, with a
     toll on it. The jumpers he never paid come for it first.
     Ending: *The Crown's Lane*.
   - **Captain Vala Marr, for the Charter** (Gate Customs): the seed is
     shut down, as the Charter says. No lane. The Lantern's last words are
     to you. Ending: *The Charter Kept*.
   - **The Lantern itself**, through Dr. Halme (Sisu Clinic): it wants to
     grow its lane up out of Revontuli Gap, where nobody owns the sky, and
     every jumper in the Gap wants it first. The lane is free to all, and
     the Lantern stays in it, talking to pilots who pass.
     Ending: *Above Everything*.
6. **First Light** (`dawn`). After a lane opens, the first landing at
   Aamunkoi Light closes the story with whoever holds it.

The game goes on after the ending: the Reach keeps its war, its trade and
its boards, with the new lane and region in it.

## The Reach's other stories

Side quests, each opened by time or by the main story, so the journal
grows as the player does:

- **Thin Air** (`air`, Lumi Farm, from day 2): Kerttu Lahja's air plant is
  failing. Bring ten machinery, then keep raiders off the farm while the
  scrubbers restart. The Cooperative remembers.
- **Old Debts** (`debts`, Vaski Works, after the claim): Harri Tuomi, a
  retired Watch pilot, never found his wingman's Kotka in Kalmisto. Find
  the wreck and bring back its flight recorder, and the last thing Lauri
  said.
- **Licence Revoked** (`licence`, Gate Customs, after Cold Light): Captain
  Vala Marr wants an AI-core runner out of Usva Deep stopped, and its
  cargo. Meet the woman who will ask for the Lantern later.
- **The Toll** (`toll`, Hopea Exchange, from day 4): Saga Virta of the
  Crown wants a stim smuggler in Tuisku Belt boarded and its manifest
  brought back. The Crown pays; the co-op notices.
- **Running Dry** (`dry`, Usva Den, from day 3): Mirja Salo's stims are
  medicine at Sisu Clinic. Run ten of them past the scanners of three
  factions that call them contraband.

## More of the Reach

Four regions joined 0.9, added after the first nine so saves keep their
numbers:

- **Tuisku Belt**: ice in a storm of grit below Vaskivuori, the co-op's
  coldest mine, Tuisku Mine. Lanes to Vaskivuori and Crown Gate.
- **Hopea Ring**: the Crown's polished outpost, Hopea Exchange, with a
  shipyard. Lanes to Lumi Field and Sisu Hollow.
- **Usva Deep**: a fog of dust past Halla Shoal where the Free Traders
  keep Usva Den, a black market. Lanes to Halla Shoal and Revontuli Gap.
- **Aamunkoi**: the new lane's end, high above everything, with Aamunkoi
  Light. Off the map until the story opens a lane to it.
